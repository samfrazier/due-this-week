Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Short version: Due This Week does not collect, sell, or share your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own. Everything it shows you comes straight from your school's learning platform to your own browser.

What the extension does

Due This Week is a browser extension that adds a weekly to-do list and progress chart to the dashboard of your school's learning management system (Canvas LMS or Blackboard).

Information the extension accesses

To show your to-do list, the extension reads, in your browser:

  • Your courses, course colors, assignments, due dates, submission and grade status, and planner items (including custom tasks you create). It reads these from your school's learning platform, using the login you already have in your browser.
  • If you turn on the optional Gradescope integration and visit gradescope.com, the extension reads the assignment list on your Gradescope course pages so it can show those assignments too.

Where that information goes

  • Only to your school's learning platform. The extension sends requests only to the website you're already using (for example, yourschool.instructure.com), and to gradescope.com if you use the Gradescope integration. It does this to load your assignments, mark items complete, and create or delete custom tasks. These are the same actions you could take on the site yourself.
  • Never to the developer or any third party. The extension has no server, sends no analytics or tracking data, and does not load code or fonts from other websites.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings (such as week start day, colors, and which features are on) are saved with the browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync feature, your browser may sync these settings between your devices. That sync is handled by your browser provider (for example, Microsoft for Edge), not by us.
  • Cached Gradescope assignments (only if you use the integration) are saved in local extension storage on your device.
  • Custom tasks you create are saved in your own learning platform account as planner notes, just like notes you create in Canvas's own planner.

Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings and cached data from your browser.

What we don't do

  • No collecting, selling, renting, or sharing of personal data
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or user IDs
  • No reading of other websites. Schools host their learning platforms on many different web addresses, so the extension starts on each page you open, checks whether it's a supported learning platform, and stops right away if it isn't.
  • No use of data for anything except showing your to-do list

Children and students

The extension is built for students and educators. Because it doesn't collect any personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not affiliated

Due This Week is an independent, unofficial extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instructure (Canvas), Anthology (Blackboard), or Gradescope.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The extension is open source, so you can review exactly what it does in the source code.
